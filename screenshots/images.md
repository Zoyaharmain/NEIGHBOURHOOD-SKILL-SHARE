# Project Screenshots

The following screenshots represent different modules and functionalities of the Neighborhood Skill Sharing platform.  
The application is designed to help users connect, learn, teach, and manage skill-sharing sessions efficiently through an interactive and user-friendly interface.

---

## Login Page
The login page allows users to securely access the platform using their registered credentials and continue their learning and skill-sharing activities.

---

## Registration Page
The registration page enables new users to create an account and join the Neighborhood Skill Sharing community.

---

## Dashboard
The dashboard provides a quick overview of user activities, skills, sessions, and pending requests through an interactive interface.

---

## My Skills
This section allows users to manage and showcase the skills they can teach or share with others.

---

## My Learning
The My Learning page helps users track the skills and sessions they are currently learning from mentors.

---

## Skills
The Skills page displays various available skills that users can explore, learn, or teach within the platform.

---

## Explore
The Explore section helps users discover new mentors, learners, and skill-sharing opportunities nearby.

---

## Manage Sessions
This page enables users to organize, schedule, and manage their booked or hosted learning sessions efficiently.

---

## Nearby
The Nearby feature helps users find skill-sharing opportunities and community members located close to their area.

---

## Exchange
The Exchange page supports collaborative learning by allowing users to exchange skills and knowledge with each other.

---

## Calendar
The Calendar section helps users keep track of upcoming sessions, schedules, and important learning activities.

---

## Chats
The Chats feature enables real-time communication between mentors and learners for better collaboration and coordination.
